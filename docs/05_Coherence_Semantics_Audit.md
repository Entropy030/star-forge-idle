# Coherence Semantics Audit

## Era I — Vacuum Coherence

`state.coherence` is gameplay-critical in Era I. It is the stability of the emerging vacuum/universe: passive stabilization adds `0.1%/s`; each Core observation adds `0.5%`, modified by the existing speed-of-light constant. Cosmic Inflation requires `100%` alongside its Quantum Fluctuation and Energy Density requirements.

## Later-era mappings retained

- **Era II:** the timeline drains Coherence above 8M K and recovers it below that threshold. This currently affects header/Codex presentation rather than Plasma command eligibility.
- **Era III:** both the timeline thermal equilibrium and stellar simulation update Coherence. The simulation's stability/fuel-efficiency/phase target is a potentially competing implementation, but it is retained because its runtime interaction has not been proven dead.
- **Era IV:** Coherence mirrors Galaxy Stability (gameplay-critical state mapping).
- **Era V:** Coherence mirrors inverse Entropy and supplies the existing Bit-production bonus (gameplay-critical).

The later mappings overload the `coherence` state name beyond the Era-I vacuum concept. This audit intentionally preserves them; the player-facing “Vacuum Coherence” identity applies to Era I only.
